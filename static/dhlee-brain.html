dhlee-brain

A personal automation agent operated by Donghyun Lee.

dhlee-brain is a single-user tool that runs on its owner's own machine. It reads and edits that person's own Google documents and calendar on their instruction — pulling a spreadsheet into a report, checking a schedule, drafting a document. It is not a product, has no other users, and is not offered as a service.

What it connects to

  • Google Sheets — read and update the owner's spreadsheets
  • Google Docs and Slides — read and edit the owner's documents
  • Google Drive — locate the owner's files
  • Google Calendar — read and manage the owner's events

Who can use it

Only the account that owns it. Authorisation is granted by that account signing in to its own Google account; no other person can connect.
